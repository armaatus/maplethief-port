# 34. Offline services: StoreKit sells nothing (every payment is cancelled), GameKit is emulated with a local file, sockets fail fast

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Issue:** #52
- **Builds on:** [ADR 0026](0026-objective-c-strategy.md) (host overrides, framework classes as host classes), [ADR 0029](0029-objective-c-runtime-services.md) (host classes, blocks ABI, the GameKit decision)
- **Supersedes:** the `+[SKPaymentQueue canMakePayments]` → NO override of ADR 0026 and ADR 0029

## Context

The game was built for an online iPhone: an in-app purchase shop (StoreKit, 10 products: `ComebackPack`, `exppack1/2`, `InitializeSP`, `mesopack3–6`, `powpackage`, `SPMarbleSP1Plus`), a Game Center leaderboard (`totalranking`), Chartboost ads, and a TCP socket (`CMSocket`) that sends purchase receipts to Nexon's server. The port runs offline. #52 has to keep the game happy without any of that, and it has to decide what the cash shop does ([ANALYSIS.md → Networking, IAP, Game Center](../ANALYSIS.md#networking-iap-game-center)).

Facts (decompiled 2026-10-27, armv7 slice; [re/objc-shell.md](../re/objc-shell.md)):

- **`canMakePayments` NO hangs the shop.** `-[EAGLView initWithCoder:]` creates `InAppPurchaseManager` only if `+[SKPaymentQueue canMakePayments]` says yes; otherwise `m_pINAPMgr` stays nil. But a purchase in the cash shop does not check it. `CStateGame::OnKeyPressed` (`0x8D4C9`) sends `-[EAGLView addLoadingView]` (`0x48C9`: a full-screen 480×320 "Please Wait..." view), sets the purchase-pending fields `CDataMgr+0x534 = 1` and `+0x538 = 7`, then `-loadProductsInfo:` to the nil manager. Only `CStateGame::Paint` (`0x87501`, cash shop case 10 and popup `0x68`) removes the view, and only when `-[InAppPurchaseManager getIsPurchaseSuccess]` returns 1 or 2. A message to nil returns 0, so the loading view stays up for ever. ADR 0026's override (and ADR 0029, which kept it) would hang every purchase attempt.
- **A cancelled purchase is a clean path the game already has.** `-paymentQueue:updatedTransactions:` (`0xBF0D5`) sends `-failedTransaction:` (`0xBF025`) for state 2 (`SKPaymentTransactionStateFailed`), which sets the result ivar to 2 and sends `-finishTransaction:`. `CStateGame::Paint` sees 2, clears `CDataMgr+0x534`, sends `removeLoadingView` and `resetIsPurchaseSuccess`. This is what a player who cancels the App Store sheet sees.
- **Invalid products leave a flag behind.** `-productsRequest:didReceiveResponse:` (`0xBE559`) with no products and invalid identifiers removes the loading view itself, but leaves `CDataMgr+0x534` set.
- **A completed purchase talks to the server.** `-completeTransaction:` (`0xBE6D9`) sends the receipt (`transactionReceipt`, `bytes`, `length`) through `CMSocket` and writes it into `totalrestore` in the save data.
- **GameKit.** The game sends `+[GameCenterManager IsGameCenterAvailable]` (`0xBF7E1`: `NSClassFromString(@"GKLocalPlayer")` and `systemVersion` ≥ 4.1), then `-AuthenticationUser` (`0xC006D`), whose completion block sets `-[EAGLView setGameCenterLoginOKorError:YES]` and calls the delegate through `dispatch_async` on the main queue. The engine reports the total-ranking score with `-ReportScore:forCategory:` (`0xBFE41`, a 64-bit value) from `CMapleWorld::changeMap` (`0x81369`) and `0x65089`. `-SubmitAchievement:PercentComplete:` (`0xBFB51`) is in the binary, but its selref (`0xFABB0`) is read only at `0xC0782`, inside its own load block: **the shipped game reports no achievement.** `-FindPlayerByid:`, `-ResetAchievements` and `-ReloadHighScoreByCategory:` have no sender. The leaderboard screen is a `GKLeaderboardViewController` that `-[EAGLView viewGameCenterRank:]` (`0x4E09`) presents modally; its delegate method (`0x4DD5`) dismisses it.
- **Sockets.** `CMSocket::Connect` (`0x1C419`) creates a `CFSocket`, connects with a 10 s timeout, and adds a run-loop source. The caller shows popup `0x20` when it fails.
- **Chartboost, `UIWebView`, SystemConfiguration, MediaPlayer.** The engine's only Chartboost sends are `+sharedChartboost` at 6 sites (`0x55360`, `0x84570`, `0x8F4D4`, `0x95406`, `0x9579C`, `0x9642A`). `UIWebView` and `SCNetworkReachability*` are used only by Chartboost. Nothing creates `MoviePlayerController`, and no video ships.

## Options considered

**StoreKit.**

1. **`canMakePayments` NO** (ADR 0026's default). No StoreKit class is ever used. But every purchase hangs on "Please Wait..." (above). It could be fixed with an override of `CStateGame::OnKeyPressed`'s shop branch, a change to the engine's own code.
2. **Products are invalid.** `-productsRequest:didReceiveResponse:` gets no products and the identifiers as invalid. The view goes away, but `CDataMgr+0x534` stays set, a state the original never reached after a purchase attempt.
3. **The store sells nothing: every payment is cancelled.** `canMakePayments` YES, the products request answers with one product, and every payment ends as `SKPaymentTransactionStateFailed`. The game takes the path of a player who cancels the App Store sheet, which it handles fully.
4. **Offline unlock.** A configuration that completes purchases. `-completeTransaction:` needs a receipt and the receipt server through `CMSocket`, and it writes receipts into the save data. That means a fake server, fake receipts in the save, and paid items for free. Out of scope, and not this port's call.

**GameKit.** (a) Override `IsGameCenterAvailable` → NO: the manager is never created and nothing is reported. (b) Emulate `GKLocalPlayer`, `GKScore`, `GKAchievement`, `GKLeaderboard` and `GKLeaderboardViewController` as host classes, with a local file. ADR 0029 already chose (b) and kept `IsGameCenterAvailable` as it is; #52 records how.

**Sockets.** (a) Fail `CFSocketConnectToAddress` at once. (b) Let it time out after 10 s, as an offline iPhone would. (c) A fake server.

## Decision

- **StoreKit: option 3.** `SKPaymentQueue`, `SKProductsRequest`, `SKProductsResponse`, `SKProduct`, `SKPayment` and `SKPaymentTransaction` are host classes (`runtime/src/stubs/storekit.cpp`). `+canMakePayments` answers YES, so `InAppPurchaseManager` is created as on the device. `-[SKProductsRequest start]` answers the delegate on the next `mt_stubs_pump` with one `SKProduct` and no invalid identifiers. `-[SKPaymentQueue addPayment:]` creates a transaction in state 0 (purchasing); on the next pump it becomes state 2 (failed) and every observer gets `-paymentQueue:updatedTransactions:` with it. `-transactionReceipt` is nil. The log says `StoreKit: the payment is cancelled: nothing is sold offline (ADR 0034)`. The `+[SKPaymentQueue canMakePayments]` → NO override is removed: `+[Chartboost sharedChartboost]` → nil is the only host override left.
- **GameKit: emulated (ADR 0029's option, now built).** `GKPlayer`, `GKLocalPlayer`, `GKScore`, `GKAchievement`, `GKLeaderboard` and `GKLeaderboardViewController` are host classes (`runtime/src/stubs/gamekit.cpp`).
  - The local player (`playerID` `G:offline`) becomes authenticated when its authentication request completes, on the next pump.
  - Scores and achievements are written to **`gamecenter.json`** in the stubs' data directory at once, and the completion handler runs on the next pump with a nil error. A score category keeps its best and last value and the number of reports; an achievement keeps its highest percentage, clamped to 0–100 (Game Center never lowers one). `+loadAchievementsWithCompletionHandler:` answers with the saved achievements (nil when there are none), and `+resetAchievementsWithCompletionHandler:` clears them.
  - The file is `{"format": "maplethief-gamecenter", "version": 1, "achievements": [{"identifier", "percentComplete"}], "scores": [{"category", "best", "last", "reports"}]}`, written to a `.tmp` file and renamed. A file that does not parse, or has another format or version, is read as empty and renamed to `gamecenter.json.bad` by the next save.
  - Leaderboards have no online scores: `-loadScoresWithCompletionHandler:` and `+loadPlayersForIdentifiers:withCompletionHandler:` answer (nil, nil). `GKLeaderboardViewController` sends its delegate `leaderboardViewControllerDidFinish:` on the next pump after `-viewDidAppear:`, so the game dismisses the screen at once. Showing the local scores there is a later UI decision.
- **The data directory** (`mt_stubs_data_dir`, `maplethief/stubs.h`) is what the platform layer sets with `mt_stubs_set_data_dir` (SDL's pref path, #54/#117). Otherwise it is `$MAPLETHIEF_DATA_DIR`, or the platform default: `~/Library/Application Support/MapleThief` (macOS, iOS), `%APPDATA%\MapleThief` (Windows), `$XDG_DATA_HOME/maplethief` or `~/.local/share/maplethief` (Linux).
- **Deferred deliveries.** Completion handlers and delegate calls never run inside the request. `mt_stubs_pump(cpu)` runs those posted before it was called, each inside its own autorelease pool, on the guest's main thread. The run loop (#54, with #48's sources) calls it each turn, next to `mt_gcd_drain_main` ([ADR 0033](0033-gcd-worker-threads-and-main-queue.md)). The stubs keep their own queue because the main queue takes guest blocks only, and most deliveries are host calls (a delegate message, a state change, then a block). A new ObjC runtime drops whatever is still queued.
- **Sockets: option (a).** `CFSocketCreate` returns a socket object that never connects. `CFSocketConnectToAddress` returns `kCFSocketError` (-1) at once and logs the timeout it skipped. `CFSocketGetNative` returns -1, `CFSocketSendData` -1, `CFSocketCreateRunLoopSource` a source that never fires, and `CFSocketInvalidate` does nothing more. The game shows its own popup `0x20`, as on a phone without network, without the 10 s wait.
- **SystemConfiguration** (Chartboost-only) is implemented anyway, as "not reachable": `SCNetworkReachabilityCreateWithAddress`/`WithName` return NULL, `GetFlags` writes 0, and scheduling and the callback return FALSE.
- **Not implemented, on purpose.** Chartboost stays ADR 0026's override; its classes, `UIWebView` and its `NSArray`/`NSDictionary` categories never run. `MPMoviePlayerController` is not a host class: `MoviePlayerController` is dead code.

## Consequences

- **Tests.**
  - In CI: `tests/cpp/stubs_test.cpp` (in `objc_test`) covers authentication on the pump; scores and achievements saved to `gamecenter.json` and read back by a new runtime (the issue's "achievements are persisted locally" test); best/last and highest-percentage rules; reset; the `.bad` file; the cancelled-payment sequence with `-finishTransaction:` and observer removal; leaderboards; the socket and reachability functions (connecting fails in under 100 ms); the data directory; and the JSON reader and writer.
  - Locally: `stubs_real_test` runs the game's recompiled `GameCenterManager` (`-AuthenticationUser` and its block, `-SubmitAchievement:PercentComplete:` with its load block, `-ReportScore:forCategory:` with a 64-bit score) and `InAppPurchaseManager` (`-init`, `-loadProductsInfo:`, then on the pumps `-productsRequest:didReceiveResponse:` and `-paymentQueue:updatedTransactions:` → `-failedTransaction:`, `getIsPurchaseSuccess` 2) against the host classes. Their delegate calls go through the runtime's `dispatch_async` and `mt_gcd_drain_main` (#50, [ADR 0033](0033-gcd-worker-threads-and-main-queue.md)). Foundation's parts are test doubles there until #49 merges. `boot_real_test` still reaches `UIApplicationMain` with one host override.
- **Other issues depend on this.** #49 has to provide what these paths send: `NSClassFromString`, `NSString` (`stringWithUTF8String:`, `UTF8String`, `compare:options:` with `NSNumericSearch`), `NSArray` (`arrayWithObject:`, fast enumeration), `NSMutableArray`, `NSMutableDictionary`, `NSSet` (`setWithObject:`) and `+[NSThread isMainThread]`. #54 has `UIDevice.systemVersion` ≥ 4.1, calls `mt_stubs_pump` each run-loop turn, makes `GKLeaderboardViewController` a `UIViewController` and sends it `-viewDidAppear:` when presented. #48 owns `CFRelease`, `CFDataCreate` and the run loop that `CMSocket::Connect` also calls with the stub's socket and source.
- **The cash shop is visible but buys nothing.** That matches a phone with In-App Purchases switched off in Settings. The 10 products stay unbought, and nothing is written to `totalrestore`.
- **How we'd know this was wrong:**
  - **A screen still waits after a cancelled purchase.** For example, another UI state than case 10 and popup `0x68` shows the loading view. Then that state needs the invalid-products answer (option 2) or an override.
  - **Players want the paid items.** Then option 4 needs its own ADR, with the receipt path and the save data.
  - **The game expects a server answer after a socket failure.** It would show as a stuck screen after popup `0x20`. Then the socket needs option (b) or (c).
