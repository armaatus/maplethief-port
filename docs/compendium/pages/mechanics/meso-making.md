# Meso making

This guide ranks the monsters by the meso a kill is worth: the meso bag it drops and what its other drops sell for at a shop. The table is built from the game's own monster and item data each time the site is made.

## Overview

A kill pays in two ways ([drops and meso](mechanics/drops.html)):

- **The meso bag:** an amount from 80% to 120% of the monster's meso value, dropped on 80% of kills.
- **Drops to sell:** every item a shop buys pays its sell price, the item table's price. Equipment drops are rare, but a piece sells for far more than most other items.

**Meso per kill** in the table is the average of both: the middle of the bag's range times 0.8, plus each drop's sell price times its real chance per kill. **Per 100 HP** divides that by the monster's HP, which favours monsters that die quickly. The chances are the real odds of the game's random numbers before the hero's level bonus, so a higher level earns slightly more.

Quest items are counted at their sell price too, though a quest that asks for them may pay more.

## Spending less

- **Stars:** a shop refills an emptied stack for one star's price times the missing stars, a small fraction of a new stack's price: a full refill of {{item:etc:11}} costs 40 meso, a new stack 170. Keep the empty stack and recharge it ([potions and scrolls](mechanics/items.html#throwing-stars)).
- **Potions** sell back for much less than they cost: {{item:etc:3}} costs 15 meso in a shop and sells for 5.
- **Dying** costs a tenth of the hero's meso on the way back to town ([death and revives](mechanics/death.html)).
