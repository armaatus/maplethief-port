# Training spots

This guide lists every map with monsters as a place to train, grouped by the level of its monsters. The tables are built from the game's own monster and map data each time the site is made.

## Overview

A map trains well when its monsters give much EXP for the HP they have, and when there are many of them. The tables rank the maps of each level band by **EXP per HP**: the EXP of all the map's spawn points divided by their HP. A higher figure means fewer hits for the same EXP. **EXP per clear** is the EXP for killing every spawn point once.

How a map really plays depends on the hero as well:

- **Monster level against ACC.** A monster evades the hero's hits with a chance of its level minus the hero's ACC in percent, and small hits also miss ([accuracy, avoid and critical hits](mechanics/accuracy.html)). A hero with ACC below the monsters' levels loses many hits.
- **Monster level against DEF.** A monster's touch hurts more the higher its level, and the hero avoids it less.
- **Layout.** Spawn points are counted, not their places; a map with its monsters on one long platform plays faster than one with many small ledges.

The bands use each map's average monster level, weighted by spawn points. Boss rooms, the tutorial and the Monster Carnival rooms are left out, and so are the bosses and the {{monster:65}}. Every map and area name links to its page, with the map's exits and its NPCs.

## Using the tables

1. Find the band that matches the hero's level, or the band below it while the hero's ACC is low.
2. Pick a map near the top of its band whose monsters are within reach of the hero's level.
3. Check the map's page for how to get there and for its exits.
