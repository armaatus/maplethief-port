# Beginner's guide

This guide covers the first hour of a new game: the tutorial, the first hunts around Henesys, the walk to Kerning City and the first job advancement at level 10. The [walkthrough](walkthrough/index.html) follows the story from there.

## Overview

A new hero starts at level 1 with 100 meso, five {{item:etc:3}}, five {{item:etc:6}} and a {{item:equip:72}} as a weapon. The two potions sit on the [short keys](mechanics/controls.html) on the right of the screen. Levels come quickly at first: level 2 needs 10 EXP and level 10 is reached after 485 EXP in all ([EXP and levelling](mechanics/exp.html)).

The game saves only from the menu (the Bag button, last tab), so save after each step below ([saving](mechanics/saving.html)). The tutorial maps are among the maps where it refuses.

## The tutorial

{{npc:14}} teaches the controls on {{map:2-8}}, one short quest at a time.

1. Talk to MacArthur: walk up to him and press up on the direction pad, or touch him.
2. Follow his quests in order: {{quest:0-main-0}}, {{quest:0-main-1}}, {{quest:0-main-2}}, {{quest:0-main-3}} and {{quest:0-main-4}}.
3. Go through the portal to {{map:2-9}} and defeat one {{monster:1}} for {{quest:0-main-5}}.
4. Return to MacArthur on {{map:2-8}} for {{quest:0-main-6}}, then leave through the portal to {{map:2-0}}.

Each tutorial quest pays 70 meso and a little EXP, so the hero reaches Henesys with about 590 meso.

## Henesys

{{map:2-0}} is the first town. Its shops are {{shop:2}}, kept by {{npc:1}} (weapons and armour), and {{shop:3}}, kept by {{npc:2}} (potions, stars and scrolls).

1. Talk to {{npc:8}} for {{quest:1-main-7}}: defeat five {{monster:2}}.
2. Go west to {{map:2-3}}, which has seven Red Snails, seven Blue Snails and four {{monster:4}}. Defeat the five Red Snails; the map's other monsters give EXP and drops for the side quests below.
3. Return to Chief Stan for the reward (81 EXP, 550 meso and three Red Potions).
4. On the way, pick up the side quests of Henesys: {{npc:7}} gives {{quest:1-sub-2}} (Tree Branches from Stumps) and {{npc:34}} gives {{quest:1-sub-3}} (Blue Snail Shells from Blue Snails). Both items drop from the monsters of {{map:2-3}}.

Before leaving, buy a few more Red Potions from Mr. Wang. Sell what the hunt dropped at either shop.

## To Kerning City

Chief Stan's next quest, {{quest:1-main-8}}, sends the hero to {{npc:3}} in {{map:0-0}}.

1. From {{map:2-0}} walk west through {{map:2-3}} to {{map:2-4}}.
2. Take the exit to {{map:0-4}}, then go on through {{map:0-3}} to {{map:0-0}}. Each of these fields has snails and Stumps.
3. Talk to the Dark Lord. {{npc:6}} in Henesys also gives {{quest:1-sub-4}}, which leads to {{npc:4}} in Kerning City.

{{map:0-0}} has its own shops: {{shop:2}} again, kept by Mr. Lee, and {{shop:5}}, kept by Mr. Wang, which also sells {{item:etc:12}}.

## The first job advancement

1. Take the Dark Lord's {{quest:1-main-21}}: defeat ten {{monster:4}}. {{map:0-1}} has eight of them and {{map:0-4}} six.
2. Return at level 10 or above. The quest rewards both first weapons, {{item:equip:24}} (a dagger) and {{item:equip:36}} (a claw), and 200 {{item:etc:11}}, and the Dark Lord gives {{job_advancement:1-1-0}}.
3. Spend the first skill points in the Bag menu's skill tab ([skills and buffs](mechanics/skills.html)). The critical rate rises from 2% to 8% with the advancement.

The choice between claw and dagger comes at the second advancement, at level 30: the claw leads to Assassin, the dagger to Bandit. Star skills need a claw and Bandit skills a dagger ([damage](mechanics/damage.html)).

After the advancement, the Dark Lord's {{quest:1-main-9}} sends the hero on to {{npc:10}} in {{map:1-0}}.

## Tips

- **Accuracy matters early.** A monster evades with a chance of its level minus the hero's ACC in percent. A level 1 hero (1 ACC) loses one hit in ten to a {{monster:2}} (level 11); each level adds ACC.
- **Monster levels run ahead.** The first monsters are level 8 to 16 in the monster table, above the hero's own level for a while ([training spots](mechanics/training-spots.html)).
- **Death is cheap before the advancement.** Below level 10, or before the first job advancement, returning to town costs a tenth of the meso and no EXP ([death and revives](mechanics/death.html)).
