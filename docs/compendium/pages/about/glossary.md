# Glossary

The game's own names, as the English and Korean tables and strings spell them, with what each one is and where it comes from. The compendium writes them exactly like this. The list is the terms table of the [style guide](https://github.com/armaatus/maplethief-port/blob/main/docs/compendium/style.md#terms); `item_etc`, `mob`, and `npc` are the data tables ([formats/data-tables.md](https://github.com/armaatus/maplethief-port/blob/main/docs/formats/data-tables.md)), and "scripts" means the strings files of the scripts.

Notes on the names:

- The tables break some NPC names over two lines with a `$`; the compendium drops it.
- Some Korean names are roles rather than names: Mr. Lee is the weapon merchant and Mr. Wang the consumables merchant.
- Two English names share one Korean name: the return scroll says Abandoned Mine, the scripts say Dead Mine.
- Other abbreviations on the pages: EXP, HP, MP, ATT, DEF, ACC, AVOID, NPC, and Lv.
