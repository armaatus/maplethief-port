Every row of the game's monster table, from the gentle {{monster:1}} of the first fields to {{monster:69}} at the end of the story. Each monster's page says where it lives, what it drops with the real odds, which quests need it or its drops, and how hard it hits.

The figures come from the monster table (`mob.dat`), the maps' spawn lists and the quest scripts, read by the compendium's knowledge base. The rules behind them (drop rolls, contact damage, avoid and evade) are those of the [mechanics notes](https://github.com/armaatus/maplethief-port/blob/main/docs/re/mechanics.md).
