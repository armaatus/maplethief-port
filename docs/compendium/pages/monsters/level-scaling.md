Monster Carnival is the one mode where a monster's level depends on the hero. When a monster is loaded during a round, the game takes half the hero's ATT plus DEF (rounded down) as a target and compares the monster's level with it:

- above the target, the level drops halfway toward it (rounded down) and the HP rises to 150%;
- below the target, the game adds half the difference instead of subtracting it, and the difference is negative, so the level falls further below the target and the HP stays as it is.

The second case looks like a sign slip in the original game: the intent was probably to raise weak monsters toward the hero, as the first case lowers strong ones. The level is what the evade, miss, avoid and contact damage rules read, so in a round a weak hero meets monsters with lower levels and, for the strong ones, more HP. A kill in a round gives no EXP, no drops and no quest progress; it adds carnival points instead.

Each monster's page works the rule out for three heroes with the base stats of levels 20, 40 and 60, one inside each round's level range.
