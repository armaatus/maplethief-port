Five monsters are bosses. Each is fought on a boss map of its own, where saving is refused and the boss's portrait and HP bar show at the top of the screen. A map script starts the fight with an entrance scene, and the boss attacks only when that scene ends. A boss's defeat starts a closing scene instead of the usual EXP and drop rolls, so its reward is the quest that asks for the kill. In version 1.5 one boss, {{monster:68}}, has no map object that runs its fight script: it has no entrance scene and fights from the hero's first moment on its map (confirmed in play). The last boss, {{monster:69}}, has no closing scene of its own: its defeat plays the game's ending.

The recommended level in the table is the compendium's estimate, not the game's: the level of the strongest monster on the maps that lead to the boss map.
