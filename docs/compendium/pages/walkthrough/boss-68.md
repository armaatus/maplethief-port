# Yeti

The quest asks for the kill, and the valley's portal opens for it. No map of version 1.5 runs the script of this fight, so there is no entrance scene and no message: the boss is one of the map's own monsters, there and fighting from the hero's first moment on {{map:9-5}} (confirmed in play, `play.chapter_2_f_synthetic`). Its defeat still plays its closing scene. The usual boss rules hold: its skill attacks cannot be avoided, and no hero of chapter 2 has the level for full ACC against it. Save in {{map:9-0}} first.
