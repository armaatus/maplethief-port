# Yeti

The quest asks for the kill, and the valley's portal opens for it, but no map of version 1.5 runs the script that starts this fight. Whether the boss appears, and how, is open until a playthrough checks it. If it does appear, the usual boss rules hold: its skill attacks cannot be avoided, and no hero of chapter 2 has the level for full ACC against it. Save in {{map:9-0}} first.
