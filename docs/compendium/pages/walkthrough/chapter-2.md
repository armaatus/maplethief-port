# Chapter 2

Chapter 2 starts on {{map:4-1}} and moves between {{area:4}}, Victoria Island, and {{area:9}}. The monsters are much stronger than in chapter 1: the fields around Orbis start at level 50 in the monster table, and the snowfields of El Nath at about 76.

The chapter has two bosses, both asked for by the main story's path: one at the far end of the gardens of Orbis and one in the valley beyond the snowfields. The second job advancement, at level 30, and the third, at level 60, are both open in this chapter from {{npc:3}} in {{map:0-0}}.

It ends with the {{npc:25}} on {{map:4-1}}, who sails to Magatia.
