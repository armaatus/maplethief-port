# The altar in the mine

{{npc:28}} gives the last quest of the story. The way runs from {{map:9-0}} through the snowfields into the mine and down to {{map:10-5}}; the portal to the altar opens only while the quest is in progress. Wear the {{item:equip:73}} for the whole descent. The [endings page](walkthrough/endings.html) says what follows the fight.
