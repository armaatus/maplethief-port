# Roy's errands and the guard

{{npc:27}} asks for a hunt on the fields of {{area:1}} and then sends the hero to {{npc:23}} in {{map:3-0}}. The way there passes {{npc:26}} on {{map:2-4}}, who keeps the portal into {{area:3}} shut until his own quest is done. He asks for an {{item:etc:106}}, and only {{npc:3}} gives one, as the reward of {{quest:1-sub-8}}.

So the part takes {{quest:1-sub-7}} from the guard, then {{quest:1-sub-8}} from {{npc:3}}, hunts the {{monster:16}}, and brings the pass back. {{quest:1-sub-8}} has a level condition: a hero of level 30 or more must have taken the second job advancement ({{quest:1-sub-16}}) before {{npc:3}} offers it, so it is simplest to take it before level 30.
