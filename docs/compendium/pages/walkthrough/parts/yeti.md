# The valley of the snowmen

{{npc:28}} sends the hero to {{map:9-5}}, beyond {{map:9-4}}, for the boss of the valley. The portal into the valley opens only while that quest is in progress and its kill is missing. The fight starts with no scene: the boss is already on the map when the hero arrives (confirmed in play, `play.chapter_2_f_synthetic`). Back in town, {{npc:28}} gives a quest that stays open until the chapter end, and the {{npc:25}} on {{map:4-1}} sails for Magatia.
