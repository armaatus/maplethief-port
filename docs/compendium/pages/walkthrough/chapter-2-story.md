# Chapter 2 story

In Orbis the {{npc:30}} brews a medicine, and the hero gathers its ingredients, one of them from the {{monster:66}} in the ruined garden. The medicine goes to {{npc:14}} in {{map:1-0}}, who then sends the hero to El Nath, where {{npc:28}} is fighting the heat and the attacks of the snowmen. The trail of the chapter ends in {{map:9-5}}, and {{npc:28}}'s next request leads to Magatia.
