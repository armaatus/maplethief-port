# Balrog

Save in {{map:9-0}}: the way down is long and saving is refused on the altar. Wear the {{item:equip:73}} for the whole descent, since the heat of the mine keeps draining HP without it. {{monster:69}} has the highest level and the most HP of the monster table, and its skill attacks hit for twice its touch damage and cannot be avoided. Bring more potions than for any fight before, and keep HP above that double hit.
