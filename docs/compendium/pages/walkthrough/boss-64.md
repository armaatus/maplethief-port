# Malady

Save in {{map:3-0}} before going down, and fill the bag with potions there. {{monster:64}} floats, and its bombs ({{monster:65}}) explode when they touch the hero, so keep moving and do not let them close in. Its skill attacks cannot be avoided and hit twice as hard as its touch, so the fight is a race between its HP and the potion supply. {{skill:5}} stops contact damage but not the boss's skill attacks ([damage](mechanics/damage.html)).
