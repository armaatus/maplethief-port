# Chapter 3 story

In Magatia the hero joins both societies of alchemists. The Zenumist side sends the hero against {{monster:67}} under its library; the Alcadno side takes the material from that fight and makes the {{item:equip:73}}. With the suit the hero can finally go down into the mine of El Nath, where {{monster:69}} waits on the altar. Its defeat is the end of the story.
