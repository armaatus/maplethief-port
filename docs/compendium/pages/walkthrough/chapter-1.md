# Chapter 1

Chapter 1 takes a new hero from the tutorial to the first job advancement and across Victoria Island: {{area:2}}, {{area:0}}, {{area:1}}, and the {{area:3}}. It is the longest chapter, with the most side quests, and the only one where the first job advancement is part of the main story.

To start, choose Play on the title screen, pick an empty slot on the character screen, and start the game. The intro scene plays and leaves the hero on {{map:2-8}} with {{npc:14}}. A new hero has level 1, 100 meso, five {{item:etc:3}}, five {{item:etc:6}}, and a {{item:equip:72}}.

The chapter has one boss, at the bottom of the {{area:3}}. It ends with the {{npc:25}} in {{map:2-0}}, who carries the hero to Orbis once his own request is done.
