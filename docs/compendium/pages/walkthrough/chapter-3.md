# Chapter 3

Chapter 3 starts in {{map:6-0}}, the town between the two societies of alchemists, the Zenumist and the Alcadno. The main story needs both: each president asks for a membership card before he helps, and the Alcadno side makes the gear for the last fight. The chapter then returns to {{area:9}} for the descent into the mine.

The chapter has two bosses: one under the Zenumist library and the last boss of the game. No chapter end follows: after the ending the hero stays in chapter 3, and no quest of it is lost. The fourth job advancement opens here, through {{quest:3-sub-25}}.
