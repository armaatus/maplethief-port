# After the ending

The save goes on in chapter 3, with every quest state as the ending left it. No chapter end follows, so nothing of chapter 3 is lost: its side quests, recipes, and trades stay open.

- {{quest:3-main-8}} stays in the quest list for good. It has nothing to do and no one to hand it to.
- The fourth job advancement is open in chapter 3, after {{quest:3-sub-25}}.
- The level-scaling [Monster Carnival](mechanics/monster-carnival.html) cannot be reached in version 1.5.
- The [checklist](walkthrough/checklist.html) lists what is still open.
