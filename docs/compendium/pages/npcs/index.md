# NPCs and shops

The NPC table (`npc.dat`) has 39 entries. Most of them stand in the towns; a few wait in fields, towers and dungeons, and three are placed on no map at all. Each NPC page says where the NPC stands and in which chapters, what it sells, crafts or trades, which quests it gives and completes, and when the game hides it.

{{npc:1}} and {{npc:2}} keep the shops of every town: {{npc:1}} sells equipment and {{npc:2}} consumables. Each shop page lists its full stock with prices, the towns where it opens and the chapters in which it does.

{{npc:12}} in {{map:0-0}} crafts equipment from minerals and jewels ([crafting](npcs/crafting.html)), and {{npc:11}}, {{npc:18}} and {{npc:31}} trade monster drops for those materials ([trades](npcs/trades.html)).

Chapters are the game's own: the tutorial is a new game until the tutorial ends, then chapters 1 to 3 follow. An NPC can stand on a map in one chapter and not in another, because the game runs a chapter's own script over the common one ([knowledge base](https://github.com/armaatus/maplethief-port/blob/main/docs/compendium/kb.md#the-quests-stage)).
