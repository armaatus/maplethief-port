# Trades

Three NPCs trade 50 of a monster drop for 3 of a crafting material: {{npc:11}} in {{map:1-0}} from chapter 1, {{npc:18}} in {{map:4-0}} from chapter 2 and {{npc:31}} in {{map:6-0}} in chapter 3. The materials are what {{npc:12}} asks for in his [recipes](npcs/crafting.html). A trader keeps the trades of earlier chapters, so each chapter's trade has its own row below.

A trade works like a recipe: it is a quest the trader prepares and completes in one conversation, and without the 50 items the conversation ends and nothing is taken.
