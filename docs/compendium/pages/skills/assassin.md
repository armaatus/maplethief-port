The Assassin is the claw branch of the thief, chosen at the second job advancement from level 30. Its attacks throw stars from a claw, so it fights from a distance and spends stars as it goes. Its skill window holds the four Rogue skills and the Assassin's tiers 2 to 4.

## Strengths

- **Range.** A star flies up to 160 pixels, while a dagger's box reaches 60 pixels in front of the hero, so most fights happen out of contact.
- **Crowds.** {{skill:10}} throws a large star that passes through 2 monsters at level 1 and 23 at level 15, hitting each with 530% at level 15.
- **Healing.** {{skill:7}} restores up to 95% of its damage as HP at level 10.
- **More stars.** {{skill:14}} doubles the stars of every throw for up to 48.5 seconds, so {{skill:2}} throws four.
- **Weak points.** Claws carry less ATT than daggers of the same level (a level 60 claw has 33 to 36, a level 60 dagger 60 to 65), which the stars make up: a star's ATT is added for each of its hits. Every throw uses stars, which cost meso to buy and to refill.
