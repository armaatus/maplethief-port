The Bandit is the dagger branch of the thief, chosen at the second job advancement from level 30. Its attacks are dagger strikes at close range, and its buffs turn meso into protection and loot. Its skill window holds the four Rogue skills and the Bandit's tiers 2 to 4.

## Strengths

- **Single targets.** {{skill:16}} strikes one monster 3 times at level 1 and 6 times from level 14, each hit with up to 95%.
- **Crowds.** {{skill:23}} hits 2 monsters at level 1 and 9 at level 20 in a box 150 pixels wide, twice each, with up to 280%.
- **Daggers.** Daggers carry far more ATT than claws of the same level, and attacks use no stars.
- **Meso.** {{skill:22}} halves every blow for a payment in meso, and {{skill:19}} makes monsters drop meso when they are hit.
- **Weak points.** Every attack is at close range, so contact damage is harder to avoid than for the Assassin, and the Bandit has no healing skill.
