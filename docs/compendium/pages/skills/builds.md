This guide suggests orders for spending skill points. They are suggestions, not rules of the game: each one follows from the skill numbers on the skill pages, and other orders work too. The two numbers that shape every build are the points a hero earns (160 by level 99) and the points every skill of a job needs (180 for the Assassin and for the Bandit), so 20 points always stay out.

## Rogue, levels 10 to 29

A Rogue earns 20 points before the second advancement.

- **Suggestion for a future Assassin:** {{skill:2}} to level 15, then {{skill:4}} with the last five points. {{skill:2}} matches {{skill:3}} at level 5 and beats it from level 6, and it stays useful with a claw after the advancement.
- **Suggestion for a future Bandit:** {{skill:3}} to level 15, then {{skill:4}}. It needs no stars, hits twice, and at levels 1 to 4 it hits harder than {{skill:2}}.
- **Either way:** one point in {{skill:5}} gives its effect for 2.5 seconds. Each further point adds 1.5 seconds, so a single point is often enough.

## Assassin, levels 30 to 59

The second advancement comes at level 30 with 60 points to spend before level 60, against 70 for all of tier 2.

1. {{skill:10}} first. At level 15 it hits with 530% and passes through up to 23 monsters, where {{skill:2}} hits one monster with two stars of 195%. Its cost is three stars and 72 MP a throw.
2. {{skill:6}} next. Each level adds 2 points of critical rate, to 30 points at level 15 (38% with the base 8%), and a critical hit does double damage, so on average every attack does 38% more.
3. {{skill:8}} adds 6% of the table's HP a level: 90% at level 15, about 1,500 HP at level 60.
4. {{skill:7}} and {{skill:9}} with the rest. {{skill:7}} heals while it hurts, and {{skill:9}} pays for the tier 3 and 4 buffs, which cost 220 MP and more.

## Assassin, levels 60 to 99

From level 60, 80 points remain for tiers 3 and 4 (55 points) and whatever is left of tier 2.

1. {{skill:11}} to level 15 for 24 ATT. The base damage grows faster than ATT (it is about ATT times ATT / 30 plus 3, over 5), so at an ATT of 60 the 24 points raise the base damage by 40%.
2. {{skill:12}} for up to 64 ATT and 44 ACC for up to 47.5 seconds.
3. {{skill:14}} once the fourth advancement opens it: twice the stars on every throw.
4. {{skill:13}} last; it deals no damage, but holds up to six monsters for up to 7 seconds.

A build along these lines leaves {{skill:3}} and most of {{skill:5}} without points.

## Bandit, levels 30 to 59

1. {{skill:16}} first: 6 hits from level 14 at up to 95% each, on one monster.
2. {{skill:15}} to 30 points of critical rate, as for the Assassin.
3. {{skill:17}} for up to 90% more HP: a close-range fighter takes more contact damage.
4. {{skill:19}} and {{skill:18}} with the rest. {{skill:19}} only adds meso, so it can wait.

## Bandit, levels 60 to 99

1. {{skill:20}} to level 15 and {{skill:21}} to level 10, for up to 24 and 64 ATT.
2. {{skill:23}} once the fourth advancement opens it: 9 monsters twice each at level 20.
3. {{skill:22}} halves every blow for meso: at level 10 a blow of 200 damage costs 81 meso and does 100. It suits a hero with meso to spare.

A build along these lines leaves {{skill:2}}, which needs a claw, and most of {{skill:5}} without points.
