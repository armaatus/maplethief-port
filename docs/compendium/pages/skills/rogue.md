The Rogue is the thief's first job, given by {{npc:3}} at the first job advancement from level 10. Its four skills are tier 1 of every thief's skill window and stay there after the second advancement, for the Assassin and the Bandit alike. Between levels 10 and 29 a Rogue earns 20 skill points, against 55 for every Rogue skill at its maximum.

## Strengths

- {{skill:2}} and {{skill:3}} are the first attacks that cost MP; both cost 6 MP at level 1 and 34 at level 15.
- {{skill:3}} needs no stars, and at low levels it is the stronger of the two: at level 1 each of its two hits uses 65%, against 41% for each of the two stars of {{skill:2}}. The two are equal at level 5, and from level 6 {{skill:2}} pulls ahead, to 195% against 135% at level 15.
- {{skill:4}} adds accuracy and avoidability, which every thief keeps using: each point of accuracy takes 1 point off a monster's chance to evade, and each point of avoidability adds 4 points to the hero's chance to avoid contact.
- {{skill:5}} makes contact harmless for a few seconds, a way out of a crowd.

## Notes

The Rogue's weapons list stops at level 29: from level 30 the second advancement settles the weapon, claws for the Assassin and daggers for the Bandit. The first advancement in chapter 1 is part of {{quest:1-main-21}}, whose rewards include a dagger and a claw.
