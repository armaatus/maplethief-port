# World

The game's world is 90 maps in 11 areas. Each area is one tile set and one project file of the game, and areas that share a place on the world map share its name: {{area:4|area 4}} and {{area:5|area 5}} are both Orbis, areas 6 to 8 are Magatia, and areas 9 and 10 are El Nath.

Maps connect through portals and through NPCs that send the hero elsewhere, such as {{npc:25}} on {{map:2-0}} and {{map:4-1}}. Every connection on these pages comes from the scripts themselves: a portal leads where its script's `changeMap` call says, and a connection with a condition (a quest state, a level or a ticket) is marked conditional with that condition. The game runs a chapter's own script over the common one, so some connections differ by chapter.

The drawings on these pages are generated from that warp graph; they are not the game's own world map.
