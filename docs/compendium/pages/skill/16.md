Strikes one monster several times in a box 60 by 24 pixels: 3 hits at level 1, 6 hits from level 14. The damage percentage is added on top of the base damage, plus 2.

## Strategy

At level 15 each of its six hits uses 95%, for 62 MP. The number of hits rises only at levels 4, 9 and 14, so those levels add the most.
