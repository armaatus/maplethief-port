A buff that halves every blow the hero takes, paying meso for it: half the damage times 90% at level 1, times 81% at level 10. It lasts 25 seconds at level 1 and 47.5 seconds at level 10.

## Strategy

At level 10 a blow of 200 damage costs 81 meso and does 100. Higher levels cost less meso per blow, so it suits a hero with meso to spare who fights at close range.
