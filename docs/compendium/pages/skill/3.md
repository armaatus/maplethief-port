Strikes one monster twice in a box 60 by 24 pixels in front of the hero. The damage percentage is added on top of the base damage, and each hit then loses 3.

## Strategy

It needs no stars. At levels 1 to 4 it hits harder than {{skill:2}} (65% against 41% at level 1), the two are equal at level 5, and from level 6 {{skill:2}} is ahead. It suits a future Bandit.
