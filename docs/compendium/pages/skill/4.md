A passive that adds the same amount to accuracy and to avoidability: 1 at level 1, 24 at level 20.

## Strategy

Each point of accuracy takes 1 point off a monster's chance to evade a hit (the chance is the monster's level minus the hero's accuracy, in percent), and each point of avoidability adds 4 points to the chance to avoid its contact. Its bonus rises by 1 or 2 a level.
