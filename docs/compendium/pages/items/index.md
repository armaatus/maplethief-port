The game has 212 items: 74 pieces of equipment for the five equipment slots and 138 other items, from potions and throwing stars to scrolls, crafting materials and quest items. Every item has a page with its stats, every way to get it, and what uses it.

Equipment found or bought always has the minimum stats of its range. Only the smiths' recipes make equipment with rolled stats, a bonus stat, and two extra upgrade slots, as {{item:equip:1}} shows.
