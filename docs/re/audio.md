# Audio: sound ids, files and the code that plays them

Which of the 44 sound files is music (BGM) and which is an effect (SFX), how a sound id becomes a file, and which code plays which id ([#29](https://github.com/armaatus/maplethief-port/issues/29)). [timing-threading.md](timing-threading.md) covers the audio-session interruption callback and [save.md](../formats/save.md) the sound settings.

Addresses are armv7 VM addresses. Function pointers carry the Thumb bit (`0x1D3B9`), and call sites and data don't. Every function and global this page names has that name in Ghidra, from `work/re/names/audio.json` (`python3 -m mtport.audio names`, `NAMES` in [`tools/mtport/audio_analysis.py`](../../tools/mtport/audio_analysis.py)), except where another names file already names it (`CMSoundPlayer::GetInstance` and the constructor in `flow.json`, `CMapleWorld::changeMap`, `CStateGame::*`, `Clet_*`).

The tables between `<!-- BEGIN generated -->` and `<!-- END generated -->` are written by `python3 -m mtport.audio generate` (run by `scripts/re-analyze`) from the bundle's `sound/` files, one `mtport.armscan` sweep of the slice and the Ghidra export. The rest is written by hand from decompiles (`tools/ghidra/run-script decompile.py OUT ADDR...`). `tests/test_audio_real.py` checks locally that the generated tables are current, that all 44 files parse and classify, that `BGM_IDS` is what `Sound_IsBGM` compares, that every call of the player is listed (armscan and Ghidra's call xrefs agree site for site), and that every id passed to the player is a constant or comes from a listed wrapper.

## Summary

- **The sound id is the file name.** Ids run 0–43. `CMSoundPlayer` formats the id with `@"%d"` and asks `[NSBundle mainBundle]` for `pathForResource:<id> ofType:@"mp3"` or `@"wav"` `inDirectory:@"sound"`.
- **BGM or SFX is decided by a hard-coded id list**, `Sound_IsBGM` `0x1CC79`: ids 0–6, 30, 31 and 34–42 are music, everything else is an effect. The list matches the files exactly: the 18 BGM ids are the 18 `.mp3` files and the 26 SFX ids are the 26 `.wav` files.
- **Music** is one `AVAudioPlayer` per id, created on first play, looped forever (`setNumberOfLoops:-1`), at volume 0.7 (or 0 while music is off). Only one track plays at a time in practice: the map, menu and scene code stop all music before they start a track. A play with *resume* = 1 continues from where that track stopped (an `AVAudioPlayer` keeps its position after `stop`), and *resume* = 0 restarts it.
- **Effects** are all decoded at start-up (`SoundPlayer_LoadEffects` `0x1D67D`) into one OpenAL buffer and one source per id, as 16-bit PCM at the file's own rate. Playing one is `alSourcePlay` on its source: fire and forget, never looped, never stopped, never positioned. Replaying a source that is still playing restarts it, so an effect never overlaps itself.
- **Who plays what**: the map's area picks the music (town, field or boss track: [Map music](#map-music)), the main menu plays 31, scenes (cut-scenes and boss intros) play their own tracks, and gameplay effects go through `Game_PlaySound` `0x80F91`. **`42.mp3` ("Flaming Racoon") is never played**: no call site and no scene passes 42. `0.mp3` plays only in the Henesys ending scene.

Format notes:

- **WAV**: all PCM 16-bit, no compression. 13 are mono 8 kHz, 11 mono 22.05 kHz, and 2 stereo 44.1 kHz (`10.wav`, `20.wav`). 13 carry extra chunks after `data` (`LIST`/`INFO`, `DISP`, an `ID3x` chunk with an ID3 tag, one `_PMX` with XMP), left there by sound editors (`12.wav`'s `INFO` says "A1 Free Sound Effects", "Water Drop"). `ExtAudioFile` ignores them, and so does the port (#62: `audio.real_wavs` decodes all 26 through the game's `MyGetOpenALAudioData` calls, 501,768 frames, each file's frame count and bytes equal to its `data` chunk).
- **MP3**: two batches. `0`–`6`, `30`, `31`, `34` and `42` are MPEG-1 Layer III, 44.1 kHz joint stereo, 128 kbit/s CBR, with an ID3v2.3 tag (empty title, encoder "Free Mp3 Wma Converter") and an ID3v1 tag at the end. `35`–`41` are MPEG-2 Layer III, 22.05 kHz joint stereo, 80 kbit/s CBR, with an ID3v2.2 tag that names the original MapleStory track (artist "Wizet"); `42` is a 44.1 kHz file with such a tag. Their first frame headers have the protection bit clear (a 16-bit CRC follows each header). No file has a Xing/Info, VBRI or LAME frame, so there are no encoder delay and padding values: a decoder that loops the track will loop the encoder's silence too, as `AVAudioPlayer` did. `AudioFile` has no packet table for them either; Apple's decoder (macOS ExtAudioFile, measured for all 18 by `audio.real_mp3`) gives MPEG frames × samples per frame of PCM, its own 529-frame decoder delay dropped and 529 frames of silence at the end ([ADR 0046](../adr/0046-avaudioplayer-mp3-streams-and-loop-points.md)).
- **Most tracks end in seconds of digital silence of their own**, so the loop point is not gapless on the device either. Measured through the game (`audio.game_sounds`, [#124](https://github.com/armaatus/maplethief-port/issues/124)) and in Apple's decode of each file (exact zero frames at the end, the 529-frame tail included, and at the start): `31.mp3`, the main menu's, has 140,985 + 5,466 zero frames, **3.32 s** of silence at its loop point; `1`, `3`, `4`, `35`–`37` and `39`–`42` have 1.7–2.8 s, `6` 0.94 s, `5` 0.25 s, and `0`, `2`, `30`, `34` and `38` only 32–120 ms. The port's loop point has exactly that silence (within one MPEG frame of Apple's zeros) and nothing more. Those silent runs are exact zeros: the leading ones are 0–6,744 frames, the trailing ones 529–141,077 (`audio.real_mp3`). A 1-LSB threshold would take 0–302 more frames from most ends, but 2.5 s (109,554 frames) of a ±1 LSB fade tail from the end of `5.mp3`. The optional `gapless_music` setting drops exactly the zero runs ([ADR 0062](../adr/0062-optional-gapless-music-loops.md), #125).
- The ID3 titles agree with the map code: the field track of each area is the original game's music for that region (36 "To Kerning City" for Kerning City, 35 "East of Henesys", 37 "PERION CASTLE (?)", 38 "Ant Tunnel" for the dungeon area, 39 "Snowy Hill" for El Nath, 40 "Dead Mine" for Zakum, 41 "Orbis Tower" for the Orbis dungeon).

## Id → file resolution

`CMSoundPlayer` (RTTI `13CMSoundPlayer`, base `CMUtil`, [cpp-classes.md](cpp-classes.md#cmsoundplayer)) is a 0x18-byte singleton. `Clet_Start` `0x5C2D` creates it at boot through `CMSoundPlayer::GetInstance` `0x1E099` (call at `0x5D18`; `new(0x18)`, global `g_soundPlayer` `0x11A460`, also reached through the pointer slot `0xF1448` → `0x11A960`, [program-flow.md](program-flow.md#clet_start-the-c-entry)). Nothing creates it again or deletes it.

| Step | Code | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `CMSoundPlayer::CMSoundPlayer` `0x1D9B1` | `CMUtil::CMUtil`, vtable `0x1196A4`. Sets `+0x04` = −1 (no BGM yet), `+0x08` = 2, `+0x10` = 0, `+0x14`…`+0x17` = 1, global `0x11A45C` = 0. Then `SoundPlayer_InitOpenAL` (call at `0x1DA7A`). |
| 2 | `SoundPlayer_InitOpenAL` `0x1D8B1` | `AudioSessionInitialize(NULL, NULL, SoundPlayer_InterruptionListener 0x1CBDD, NULL)` at `0x1D8D2`. `alcOpenDevice(NULL)` `0x1D8DA`, `alcCreateContext` `0x1D8F6` → `g_alContext` `0x11ACC4`, `alcMakeContextCurrent` `0x1D91A`. `alGenBuffers(44, g_soundBuffers 0x11AB50)` `0x1D92C` and `alGenSources(44, g_soundSources 0x11AAA0)` `0x1D968`; either error prints and calls `exit(1)`. Then `SoundPlayer_LoadEffects` (`0x1D99C`) and `SoundPlayer_AttachBuffers` (`0x1D9A0`). |
| 3 | `SoundPlayer_LoadEffects` `0x1D67D` | For each id 0–43 where `Sound_IsBGM(id)` is false: `[NSString stringWithFormat:@"%d", id]` (`0x1D6FC`; CFString `0x117F54` → `"%d"` `0xE3736`), `[[NSBundle mainBundle] pathForResource:ofType:inDirectory:]` (`0x1D720`) with `@"wav"` (`0x117F84` → `0xE3777`) and `@"sound"` (`0x117F74` → `0xE373D`), `[[NSURL alloc] initFileURLWithPath:]` (`0x1D754`), then `MyGetOpenALAudioData(url, &size, &format, &rate)` (call `0x1D760`). A pending AL error prints `"error loading %s: "` with `g_soundNames[id]` (`0x11AC10`, a never-written `char*[44]`, so NULL) and calls `exit(1)`. Otherwise `alBufferData(g_soundBuffers[id], format, data, size, rate)` at `0x1D834` and `free(data)`. The `id == 0` branch copies the data to `g_soundStaticData` `0x11ACC0` and hands it to `alBufferDataStaticProc` `0x1D5F5` instead; it can't run, because 0 is a BGM id. |
| 4 | `MyGetOpenALAudioData` `0xA0045` | Apple's oalTouch sample code (`lib:apple-openal-support`, [libraries.md](libraries.md)). `ExtAudioFileOpenURL`, reads the file format (`'ffmt'`), warns on more than 2 channels, sets the client format to linear PCM (`'lpcm'`, flags `0xC` = signed integer, packed, native endian), 16 bits, the file's channel count and sample rate, reads the length (`'#frm'`) and the whole file in one `ExtAudioFileRead`. Format: `AL_FORMAT_MONO16` (`0x1101`) for 1 channel, `AL_FORMAT_STEREO16` (`0x1103`) for 2. On failure it prints one of the six `MyGetOpenALAudioData` messages and returns NULL, which step 3 still passes to `alBufferData`. |
| 5 | `SoundPlayer_AttachBuffers` `0x1D555` | `alSourcei(g_soundSources[i], AL_BUFFER, g_soundBuffers[i])` (`0x1D5A4`) for all 44 ids, BGM ids included (their buffers stay empty). | 6 | `CMSoundPlayer::LoadBGM` `0x1D119` | Called by `Play` the first time a BGM id plays. `Sound_IsBGM(id)` must hold. `[[NSString alloc] initWithFormat:@"%d", id]` (`0x1D176`), `pathForResource:ofType:inDirectory:` (`0x1D1C4`) with `@"mp3"` (`0x117F64` → `0xE3739`) and `@"sound"`, `[[NSURL alloc] initFileURLWithPath:]` (`0x1D1F0`), `[[AVAudioPlayer alloc] initWithContentsOfURL:url error:nil]` (`0x1D208`, `0x1D21E`). `setVolume:` 0.7 if music is on (`+0x17`), else 0 (`0x1D240`/`0x1D254`), `setNumberOfLoops:-1` (`0x1D26C`), `prepareToPlay` (`0x1D280`). So the resolution is `sound/<decimal id>.<mp3 if Sound_IsBGM(id) else wav>` inside the app bundle, with no table, no language variant and no fallback. A missing `.wav` ends the app at start-up; a missing `.mp3` gives a nil player and silence.

### Playing: `CMSoundPlayer::Play(this, id, resume)` `0x1D3B9`

In order, `Play`:

1. stores *resume* in a global (`0x11A458`) that nothing reads;
2. for an effect (`!Sound_IsBGM(id)`, `0x1CC79`), calls `SoundPlayer_PlayEffect(id)` (`0x1D391`, which is `alSourcePlay` on the effect's source) and returns;
3. for music, records *id* at `this+0x04` (read by `ResumeBGM`), and loads the track with `LoadBGM` (`0x1D119`) if `CMResMgr+0x590 + 4·id` holds no player yet;
4. if there is a player: stops it if it is playing (`0x1D486`, `0x1D4A8`), rewinds it to 0 unless *resume* (`0x1D4C8`), sets its volume to 0.7 if music is on and 0 otherwise (`0x1D4E8` / `0x1D4FC`), and calls `play` (`0x1D50C`).

`Play` doesn't stop the other tracks; its callers do (`StopAllBGM` before every music change). The effects ignore the *resume* flag.

### Vibration

The phone API's vibrate call survives as part of the player. `CMSoundPlayer::Vibrate(this, ms)` `0x1D2E9` calls `AudioServicesPlaySystemSound(kSystemSoundID_Vibrate = 0xFFF)` (`0x1D2FE`) if the vibration flag `+0x14` is set; the duration is ignored. `VibratePreview` `0x1D309` is `Vibrate(500)`, used when the option is switched in the options menu. `SetVibrate` `0x1CC61` sets `+0x14`; `FUN_00037bd0` `0x37BD1` applies config byte 3 ([save.md](../formats/save.md#config): 0 = off) through it and through `SetVibrateParam0C` `0x1CC11` and `SetVibrateParam10` `0x1CC39` (store the byte in `+0x0C`/`+0x10`, nothing reads them). `StopVibrate` `0x1CC71` is empty.

## Who plays what

### Map music

`CStateGame::PlayMapBGM` `0x81031` picks the music of the current map from the game state's area (`+0x84`) and map (`+0x88`, the fields of [mapprj.md](../formats/mapprj.md)). `CStateGame::Paint` calls it (`0x87FF4`) when the flag `+0x3FC` is set (presumably by the map load; `PlayMapBGM` clears it), and `CStateGame::OnKeyPressed` calls it once at `0x8FE98`. It first calls `StopAllBGM`, then:

1. **Town maps** (map 0 of every area, plus Henesys maps 8 and 9): the area's town track, **restarted** (resume 0). An area outside 0–10 would play effect 9 instead (no such area ships).
2. **Boss maps** (3, 8), (4, 17), (7, 4), (9, 5) and (10, 5) (the last four are the maps the camera follows 32 px higher, [mapprj.md](../formats/mapprj.md)): track 34, resumed.
3. **Every other map**: the area's field track, **resumed** from where it stopped, so walking between field maps doesn't restart the music. Areas 4–8 use their town track here too, so their music only restarts on map 0.

`TOWN_BGM`, `BOSS_MAPS` and `FIELD_BGM` in `mtport.audio` transcribe the function, and the generator checks that the transcription produces exactly the (id, resume) pairs of its 20 `Play` calls. The table applies it to the 90 shipped maps (map names split as in [map.md](../formats/map.md)):

Checked through the recompiled function ([#124](https://github.com/armaatus/maplethief-port/issues/124)): `audio.game_map_music` calls `PlayMapBGM` for all 90 shipped maps in turn (only `+0x84`, `+0x88` and the flag `+0x3FC` of `this` are touched) and the trace gives this table exactly: 13 restarts (`setCurrentTime:0`, `play` at frame 0) and 77 resumes, each resumed track going on from the frame where `StopAllBGM` stopped it. An area outside 0–10 plays effect 9. In the game itself, a new game's intro scene plays 30 (restarted) and, after its fifth speech box, the first playable map (Henesys) starts 5 restarted; tapping through MacArthur's dialog there played effect 12 four times (the trace of the soak run, `alSourcePlay(13)`).

A warp goes through `CMapleWorld::changeMap` `0x81369` (from the script native `changeMap`, [script-vm.md](script-vm.md)): `StopAllBGM` (`0x813AE`), then effect 9 (`0x813C4`), the warp sound. The next map's music starts when `PlayMapBGM` runs after the load. (Its `this` has the area and map at `+0x98`/`+0x9C` like `CStateGame`, so the `CMapleWorld::` in #19's name is probably the wrong class.)

### Main menu

`CStateMainMenu::LoadCharacterSlots` `0x96845` stops all music and plays 31 restarted (`0x9687A`); `CStateMainMenu::OnKeyPressed` `0x9462D` plays 31 with resume 1 (`0x94890`, `0x96574`; `StopAllBGM` at `0x94874`) and effects 7 and 43 on keys. `CStateGame::CStateGame` `0x940D9` stops all music when the game starts. `CStateTitle` plays nothing.

### Scenes

The cut-scenes and boss intros (`runAnimation`, `startBoss`, [script-vm.md](script-vm.md)) are command lists that the `CAniManager::makeAni*` builders fill with `Scene_AddCommand` `0x26809` `(list, step, command, p1, p2)`, `Scene_AddCommandP2` `0x26895` (p1 = 0) and `Scene_AddCommand0` `0x268C5` (p1 = p2 = 0). `CStateGame::PaintExitToMenu` `0x83C71` (#12's name for it; it is the scene runner that `CStateGame::Paint` calls every frame) executes the commands of the current step with a `switch (command − 1)`, reading p1 and p2 through `Scene_ArgP1` `0x26981` and `Scene_ArgP2` `0x269C9`. ### Effects

Gameplay code plays effects through `Game_PlaySound(game, id)` `0x80F91`, which calls `Play(player, id, 0)` (`0x81028`). It skips the call when the current BGM is 29 or 8, the id equals it and the byte at `*0xF13A8` is 1; the current BGM is always a BGM id or −1, so that guard never fires (it looks like a leftover of looping effects on the phone version). The menu, interaction and key handlers call `Play` directly. The meaning of each effect isn't settled by the code alone; what is known from the callers:

- 7: confirm/select, the most common (menus, `CHero::InteractWithObject`, 15 sites in `CStateGame::OnKeyPressed`). 12: another key sound in `CStateGame::OnKeyPressed`, also played by `FUN_00061650` `0x61651` and `FUN_00063664` `0x63665`.
- 9: warp (portal) in `CMapleWorld::changeMap`.
- 16, 17, 18: probably a mob being hit, picked by its kind (`FUN_00073714` `0x73715` takes damage off `+0x88` and switches on `+0xA0` before `0x73B20`…`0x73B3C`). Its double-damage branch (a critical hit on the mob) also vibrates for 100 ms (`0x73888`).
- 32: a quest requirement completed (`CQuestList::checkQuestRequireMob`, `CQuestList::checkQuestRequireItem`).
- 13–15, 19–28, 33: probably skill effects (`CSkill::setActiveSkill` `0x7EFF1` and its neighbours `FUN_0007d780` `0x7D781`, `FUN_0007f748` `0x7F749`).
- 8, 10, 11, 29: gameplay functions: 8 an item used (`FUN_00066694` `0x66695`, a potion's HP and MP), 10 the hero's death (`CHero::Die` `0x65979`), 11 the hero's draw (`CHero::Paint` `0x61A19`, with a level-up's white flash frame, [flashes.md](flashes.md)), 29 unnamed (`FUN_0005bc74` `0x5BC75`). `CHero::TakeDamage` `0x65B55` (`hero, damage, from_left`; nothing for a dead hero, "miss" for `damage <= 0`) vibrates for 100 ms (`0x65C18`) on every hit, then subtracts the damage (halved by a guard skill) from HP `+0x94`. Its callers are `CHero::CheckMobContact` `0x65F05` and `CHero::CheckAttackHit` `0x65DC5`. A level-up (`CHero::LevelUp` `0x65089`) doesn't vibrate. The port rumbles the gamepad on the vibration and on both events ([ADR 0053](../adr/0053-observed-functions-game-events-and-rumble.md)).
- 43: main-menu key sound.

## For the port

- One `PlayMusic(id, resume)` / `StopAllMusic()` pair covers every music call. `resume` must keep the stopped track's position, which `AVAudioPlayer` did implicitly. Loop forever; the MP3s have no gapless data, so loop exactly what the decoder produces (the port: Apple's timeline, [ADR 0046](../adr/0046-avaudioplayer-mp3-streams-and-loop-points.md)).
- Effects: decode all 26 WAVs at start-up (or lazily), one voice per id, restart on replay, no looping. A master gain of 0/1 for the effects setting, a music volume of 0/0.7 for the music setting.
- Config byte 0 is a 2-bit mask (bit 0 effects, bit 1 music); config byte 3 switches vibration, which maps to the gamepad's rumble (`settings.ini`'s `rumble` gates every rumble on top; [ADR 0053](../adr/0053-observed-functions-game-events-and-rumble.md)).
- The audio session calls (category ambient, interruptions) have no equivalent on desktop and can become no-ops.
